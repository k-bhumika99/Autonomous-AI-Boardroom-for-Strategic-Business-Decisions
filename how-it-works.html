How AI Boardroom Works

From business dilemma to quantitative sign-off in seven transparent, connected steps.

01
✏️

1. Enter Business Scenario

Input your strategic proposition, target market, expected investment capital, projected revenue, and estimated operating expenses.

02
💰

2. Finance Analysis

The CFO agent stress-tests net margins, capital intensity, break-even milestones, payback horizons, and long-term liquidity impact.

03
📣

3. Marketing Analysis

The CMO agent interrogates market sizing, addressable customer segments, go-to-market channels, CAC/LTV benchmarks, and brand positioning.

04
⚙️

4. Operations Analysis

The COO agent examines supply-chain resiliency, execution bandwidth, delivery bottlenecks, key personnel requirements, and scalability.

05
⚠️

5. Risk Assessment

The CRO agent flags regulatory liabilities, competitive counter-moves, compliance demands, macroeconomic volatility, and downside mitigations.

06
🗣️

6. Executive Review

An active boardroom debate pits optimistic growth hypotheses against conservative downside defenses to surface hidden contradictions.

07
📜

7. Boardroom Decision

The synthetic CEO issues the final authoritative recommendation, approved budget allocation, conditional covenants, and strategic roadmaps.
